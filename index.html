Discover

The Jewel of Kumaon

Nestled at an elevation of 2,084 metres in the Kumaon Himalayas of Uttarakhand, Nainital is one of India's most enchanting hill stations — a landscape of emerald hills, dense oak forests, and a sparkling pear-shaped lake that mirrors the sky.

The legendary Naini Lake is the city's beating heart, perfect for a serene boat ride at dawn. Stroll along the beloved Mall Road lined with colonial-era architecture, vibrant markets, and charming cafés. Trek up to Tiffin Top for panoramic Himalayan vistas, or visit the revered Naina Devi Temple perched beside the lakeside ghats.

With its cool mountain breeze year-round, colourful festivals, cable-car rides over the valleys, and star-lit skies — Nainital promises an experience that stays with you long after you leave.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times? +

Check-in time is 12:00 noon and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to room availability and may be arranged on request (additional charges may apply). Luggage storage is complimentary.

Is parking available at the hotel? +

Yes, we offer complimentary secure parking for all registered guests within our premises. The parking area is monitored by CCTV. Please inform us in advance if you are arriving with a larger vehicle.

Is breakfast included in the room rate? +

Breakfast inclusion varies by package. Our Super Deluxe and Family Suite bookings on the MAP (Modified American Plan) include complimentary breakfast. For Deluxe rooms, breakfast can be added for ₹350 per person per day. Please check your booking details or contact us to confirm.

Is the hotel close to Mall Road and Naini Lake? +

Absolutely! Hotel Sudarshan is ideally located on Mall Road, the heart of Nainital — just a short walk from the Naini Lake boating ghats, the Naina Devi Temple, local markets, and popular restaurants. Guests can explore all major attractions on foot.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Cancellations made 48 hours or more before check-in are fully refunded. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in are subject to a one-night charge. During peak season (May–June and December), a 72-hour cancellation policy applies. No-shows will be charged the full booking amount.

Do you offer travel or tour packages? +

Yes! Our concierge team offers curated day trips, trekking arrangements, boat ride bookings on Naini Lake, and guided tours to nearby attractions like Bhimtal, Mukteshwar, and Corbett National Park. Ask our front desk for current packages and rates.
