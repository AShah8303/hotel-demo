Discover

The Jewel of Kumaon

Perched at 2,084 metres in the Kumaon Himalayas of Uttarakhand, Nainital is one of northern India's most beloved hill stations — a world of pine-scented forests, colonial promenades and a shimmering, pear-shaped lake that has captivated travellers for centuries.

The legendary Naini Lake is the soul of the town — serene at dawn, alive with colour by afternoon. Walk the length of Mall Road past Victorian-era facades and vibrant market stalls, take the cable car to Snow View Point for sweeping Himalayan panoramas, or seek blessings at the revered Naina Devi Temple perched at the lake's edge.

With a mild, pleasant climate year-round, lively festivals, and a natural landscape of rare quiet beauty, Nainital leaves a mark on every visitor that time cannot erase.

Our Story

A Home Above the Hills of Nainital

Set on Zoo Road, Tallital — just steps from the Naini Lake and the heart of Nainital — Hotel Sudarshan has long been a trusted retreat for families, couples and solo travellers seeking genuine comfort at an honest price. The hotel is known for its attentive staff, clean well-kept rooms and an address that puts the best of Nainital right at your door.

With complimentary Wi-Fi, free on-site parking, 24-hour room service, daily housekeeping, cable television in every room, and taxi and tour assistance available at the front desk — Hotel Sudarshan offers everything you need to settle in and explore at your own pace.

Questions & Answers

Things Guests Often Ask

What are the check-in and check-out times? +

Check-in at Hotel Sudarshan begins at 10:00 AM and check-out is at 10:00 AM. The front desk is staffed 24 hours a day, so early arrivals are welcome to leave luggage. Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged based on availability — please contact the hotel directly to request this.

Where exactly is the hotel located? +

Hotel Sudarshan is located at Zoo Road, Tallital, Nainital — 263002, Uttarakhand, India. The hotel sits in Tallital, the southern end of town, close to the Nainital Zoo and within easy reach of the Naini Lake, Mall Road, and the Naina Devi Temple. Most major attractions are walkable from the property.

Is parking available at the hotel? +

Yes. Hotel Sudarshan offers free on-site private parking for all registered guests. This is one of the hotel's noted conveniences, as parking in Nainital can be difficult during peak season. Please inform the hotel in advance if you are arriving with a larger vehicle.

Is breakfast included in the room rate? +

Breakfast may be included depending on the rate plan selected at the time of booking. The hotel does not have an on-site restaurant, but dining options are readily available on nearby Mall Road and in Tallital market. Please confirm breakfast inclusion with the hotel when booking.

Does the hotel have a restaurant or swimming pool? +

Hotel Sudarshan does not have an on-site restaurant or a swimming pool. However, 24-hour room service is available, and the hotel is surrounded by a wide variety of restaurants, dhabas and cafés along Mall Road and Tallital that cater to every taste and budget.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Hotel Sudarshan offers free cancellation on most bookings, subject to the rate plan chosen. Cancellation terms may vary by season and booking platform. Guests are advised to confirm the exact cancellation policy at the time of reservation, as peak season bookings (May–June, October and December) may carry different conditions.

Are children and pets welcome? +

Hotel Sudarshan is a family-friendly property and warmly welcomes children. The hotel is considered suitable for families and has been reviewed positively by families travelling with children. Pets may be allowed upon request — please contact the hotel directly to confirm before your arrival.

Is taxi or transport available from the hotel? +

Yes. The hotel provides taxi and transport assistance for guests. The front desk can help arrange local transfers, sightseeing trips to Bhimtal Lake, Mukteshwar, Corbett National Park, and other nearby attractions. Cab availability and rates can be confirmed directly with the hotel team.
